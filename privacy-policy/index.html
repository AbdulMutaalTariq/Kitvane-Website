Privacy Policy
Transparency & User Protection

Last Updated: October 2026. This Privacy Policy outlines how Kitvane collects, manages, and protects your information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Kitvane ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your fundamental right to digital privacy. This policy explains the nature of our client-side software utilities, the limited technical analytics we gather, our utilization of cookies, and your legal data privacy rights.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

100% Client-Side Architecture: All processing operations for tools hosted on Kitvane occur strictly within your device's browser memory via JavaScript.

Specifically, we never capture, intercept, or upload:

  • Image files or photos loaded into the Image Compressor.
  • Financial metrics, principal balances, or loan terms keyed into the EMI Calculator.
  • Birthdates or milestones input into the Age Calculator.
  • Text fragments or passwords typed into the Fancy Text or Word Finder utilities.

3. Data We Do Collect & Third-Party Services

To keep Kitvane operational and free for all global visitors, we rely on standard third-party web services:

A. Anonymized Web Analytics (Google Analytics): We monitor aggregate engagement metrics (such as visited URLs, duration on site, browser type, and general geographic country). IP addresses are masked and anonymized before processing.

B. Display Advertising (Google AdSense): We serve contextual banner ads through Google AdSense. Google and third-party advertising partners may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on prior visits to our site or other web destinations. Users may customize or opt out of personalized ad tracking by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Cookie Disclosure

Cookies are small text records stored on your hard drive. Below is an inventory of cookies used on our domain:

Cookie Type Provider Functional Purpose Retention
Analytics Google Analytics Measures site traffic patterns and popular tools Up to 14 months
Advertising Google AdSense / Partners Delivers relevant advertising and manages frequency Up to 24 months
Essential / Login None We have no user login or account database None

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Kitvane is an open, family-friendly educational and utility platform. However, our services are not aimed directly at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly harvest personally identifiable details from minors.

6. Your Global Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location (such as the European Economic Area or California), you are entitled to certain consumer rights:

  • Right to Know / Access: The right to confirm whether personal data is processed.
  • Right to Erasure: The right to demand deletion of personal information. (Because Kitvane maintains no user accounts or persistent database records, we hold zero user-identifiable profiles to delete).
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale: We do not sell or monetize personal customer records.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or formal concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please email our privacy representative at hello@kitvane.link.
