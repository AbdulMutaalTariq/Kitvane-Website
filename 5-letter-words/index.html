How to Use the 5-Letter Word Finder for Wordle

Wordle provides feedback through three distinct colored tiles after each guess. Our filter corresponds directly to these clues:

G

Green Tiles (Correct Letter in Correct Position)

Use Starts With if your green letters are at the beginning (e.g. ST), or Ends With if they are at the end (e.g. ER).

Y

Yellow Tiles (Correct Letter in Wrong Position)

Enter all yellow letters into Must Contain. For example, if you know the word contains both A and R, type AR.

X

Gray Tiles (Letters Not in the Word)

Enter every eliminated gray letter into Exclude Letters. If you tested CRANE and only A was yellow, put CRNE in the exclude box.
