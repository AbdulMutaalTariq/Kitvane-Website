How Does This Age Calculator Work?

Most basic calculators estimate age by subtracting year numbers or dividing total elapsed days by 365.25. While that provides a ballpark figure, it leads to errors because calendar months vary in length (28 to 31 days) and leap years occur every four years.

Our tool uses true calendar date arithmetic. It first measures the number of full calendar years elapsed. Next, it steps through each specific calendar month to determine remaining months, and finally tallies the leftover days based on the exact number of days in the preceding month. This ensures 100% calendar precision down to the exact day.

Fascinating Biological & Chronological Age Facts

  • Heartbeats: The average human heart beats around 70 to 80 times per minute. By your 30th birthday, your heart has pumped more than 1.1 billion times.
  • Sleep Duration: By age 45, an individual will have spent roughly 15 full continuous years sleeping and dreaming.
  • Leap Year Babies (Leaplings): People born on February 29 legally celebrate non-leap year birthdays on March 1 in most Western jurisdictions, or on February 28 in select European registries.
  • Record Longevity: The oldest verified person in recorded human history was Jeanne Calment of France, who lived to an extraordinary age of 122 years and 164 days (44,724 days).

Traditional Korean Age vs. International Age Explained

In the traditional East Asian age reckoning system (prominently used in South Korea until official reforms), a newborn was considered 1 year old on the day of birth because gestational time in the womb was counted. Furthermore, everyone gained an additional year collectively on January 1st (New Year's Day) rather than on their individual birth anniversary.

Under this system, a baby born on December 31 was deemed 1 year old at birth, and turned 2 years old the very next morning on January 1! In June 2023, South Korea officially adopted the international age standard for government, administrative, and legal matters to eliminate confusion in international commerce.
